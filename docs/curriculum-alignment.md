## Algebra - Matematika Fase D

The Level 1 sequence is anchored to the guide's Fase D progression across generalizing object/number patterns, representing algebraic forms, relations and functions, and linear/nonlinear representations. The demo begins with concrete growing patterns, moves from a placeholder to a variable, and only then compresses visual groups into an algebraic expression.

Relevant source PDF pages visually reviewed: 148-155 (printed pages 142-149).

## Calculus - Matematika Tingkat Lanjut Fase F

The Level 1 sequence is an accessible conceptual bridge into the guide's Limit, Turunan, dan Integral strand. It follows the stated prerequisite relationship from limit to rate of change and derivative, beginning with average change, approaching a single instant, interpreting instantaneous speed, and treating the derivative as a function. Formula memorization is not the entry point.

Relevant source PDF pages visually reviewed: 245-246 (printed pages 239-240).
